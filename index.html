General
Algo trading (algorithmic trading) is a method where buy and sell decisions are made automatically by a computer program based on pre-defined rules — price levels, indicators, time conditions. There is no human emotion or discretion involved. The system executes the same logic every day, every trade, without hesitation or bias.
Algo Tamil is an algorithmic trading strategy platform by Mahesh, sharing daily points and a 1-year backtest of rule-based strategies across Nifty, Bank Nifty and Sensex. All strategies are system-generated — no emotions, no discretion.
Buying strategies have been live since October 2023. Selling strategies since January 2025. As per SEBI norms, the site shows only today's triggered strategies (in points) and a hypothetical 1-year backtest with slippage.

Maximum 3 strategies can trigger simultaneously — and only CE side or PE side triggers at a time, never both. So maximum capital used at any point is ₹45,000 (3 × ₹15,000). The remaining ₹10,000 is kept as a buffer for drawdown management.
Each buying strategy has a defined stop loss per trade. The system exits automatically when the stop loss is hit — no manual intervention needed. Stop loss levels are defined per strategy.
CE side strategies do not have fixed targets — the position is trailed or exited at stop loss. PE side strategies have defined targets. This asymmetric approach maximises gains on trending moves while controlling downside.
Buying strategies average 15–20 trading days per month. Selling strategies maintain positions with entry, adjustment and exit levels as per market conditions.
